The native binary still depends on a supported system ABI and C++ standard library. Android's SDK/NDK, operating system APIs, debug signing tools and delivery infrastructure are independently maintained by their vendors. Removing an intermediary does not remove platform dependency or certify government procurement compliance. A contract-by-contract supply-chain and legal review remains necessary.

Only tag-gated, checksum-verified numerical releases may publish. CI Android output is temporary debug-signed, never a production-signed or device-certified release. There are no bundled trained speech weights, model transcripts or automatic translations.
